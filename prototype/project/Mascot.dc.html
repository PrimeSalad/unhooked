Meet Ginto

Ginto is Filipino for gold. A small goldfish who keeps swimming past the hook, Ginto never scolds. Ginto reacts to the moment so the user feels accompanied, not watched.

Round, never sharp
Every shape is a soft curve. Only the hook has a point.
Eyes do the acting
Mood comes from eyes, brows and mouth. The body stays the same.
One Ginto per screen
Ginto swims between screens instead of popping in.
Never judges
No angry face, no crying, no pointing at money.
